Methodology

Our 4-Stage Turnkey Process

A disciplined, transparent delivery framework engineered to eliminate guesswork.

01 Concept & Mood
Week 1-3

Spatial spatial audits, architectural zoning, 3D atmospheric mood boards, and sensory material tactile sampling in our studio.

02 Detailed Engineering & CAD
Week 4-6

Millwork shop drawings, full MEP & HVAC coordination, fixture schedule approvals, and locked-down transparent bills of quantities (BOQ).

03 Factory Fabrication
Week 7-11

Precision CNC cutting, timber seasoning, custom stone milling, and metalwork finishes crafted concurrently in controlled workshop environments.

04 White-Glove Installation
Week 12-14

Site fit-out, acoustic testing, architectural lighting calibration, professional deep sanitization, and sensory walkthrough with the lead architect.
