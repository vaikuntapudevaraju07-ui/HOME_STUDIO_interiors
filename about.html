Our Lineage

Decade of Restraint

2014 — 2025

From a two-person sketchbook studio in 2014 to an international team of 35 architects, interior designers, and master carpenters. Over a decade, we have remained devoted to honest materiality, uncluttered layouts, and timeless craftsmanship over fleeting trends.

Our Methodology

Design Process

A meticulous, six-stage evolution transforming initial intention into living poetry.

01

Consultation

Lifestyle discovery, site analysis, and spatial goals.

02

Planning

Zoning, flow ergonomics, 2D blueprints, and architectural layouts.

03

Design

3D photorealistic renderings, mood boards, and lighting simulations.

04

Material Selection

Guided atelier visit to touch veneers, stones, and tactile fabrics.

05

Execution

Civil changes, MEP integration, artisan joinery, and on-site oversight.

06

Handover

Deep clean, white-glove styling, and seamless keys-in-hand delivery.
